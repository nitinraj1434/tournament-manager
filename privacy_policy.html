Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

Tournament Manager ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using our app:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture (via Google Sign-In)
  • Payment Information: UPI ID, bank account details (for wallet transactions)
  • Tournament Data: Game IDs, tournament participation records

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, time spent on app, interaction patterns
  • Network Information: IP address, network provider

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Processing tournament registrations and payments
  • Sending notifications about tournaments and wallet activities
  • Improving app functionality and user experience
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring platform security
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

We use Firebase services (Google Cloud) to store and process your data:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication
  • Cloud Firestore: User profiles, tournaments, and transactions
  • Firebase Storage: Profile pictures and tournament images
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notifications

Security Measures: We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure authentication, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share information only in these cases:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google), payment processors (for wallet transactions)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Tournament Participants: Your username and game ID may be visible to other participants

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Export your data in a portable format

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication (Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase: For backend services (Privacy Policy)

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request account deletion at any time through the app settings.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Cookies and Tracking

Our app does not use cookies. However, third-party services (like Firebase Analytics) may use similar technologies to collect usage information.
